About Me

UX & Digital Design

A UX and digital designer with over ten years’ experience across web, product and digital design. At Asda, I was responsible for the UX design and build of supplier-funded pages across the website and app. I worked in the team that ran all funded media on the site, for global brands including P&G, Nestlé and Coca-Cola. I’m now Lead Digital Designer at Infoserve, leading UX and web projects from research to launch.
