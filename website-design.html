Healthcare · WordPress · Branding

JL Dental Care

Trade · WordPress · Redesign

Oakfield Decoration

A professional, polished website built alongside a rebrand — giving Oakfield a cohesive identity that properly reflects the quality of their work. Clean layout, strong imagery, and clear messaging make a strong first impression with the commercial and domestic clients they work with across London.

Redesign · WordPress · Motion

Tigon Scaffolding

I redesigned and rebuilt the Tigon Scaffolding website after their original site had gradually become cluttered and unfocused. Estate Agency · WordPress · CRM Integration

A visual overhaul for this estate agency, with a clear focus on driving valuations. I integrated the site with their existing property CRM so listings stay current without manual updates, and redesigned the experience to guide visitors naturally towards booking a valuation — making the site work harder as a lead generation tool.

The existing site was dense, hard to navigate, and difficult for most visitors to make sense of. Alongside a full rebrand, the redesign strips it back to what matters — clear structure, plain-English explanations, and an intuitive layout that makes it easy to find the right service and get in touch.
