01 · Pushback

Working through pushback with the client

1

Email first or price first?

The client wanted the email before any price. Competitors and the effort it adds said price first. Too few orders for an A/B test, so we launch price first, measure saved quotes, and keep email first ready to test.

2

"Why £30 for my own box?"

People couldn't see what it paid for. Renamed "Pack and present" to show the expert packing.

3

Charges that felt like penalties

Admin fee removed, the bag shown as included, and follow-up emails added for saved quotes.
